Methodology & Precision

Our Curatorial Process

From initial spatial concept to white-glove handover, a meticulous 4-stage journey.

01Weeks 1–3

Dialogue & Spatial Discovery

We conduct an intimate lifestyle audit: analyzing your daily domestic rituals, natural daylight angles, acoustic patterns, and functional priorities.

  • Acoustic & Daylight Study
  • Functional Ritual Matrix
  • Preliminary Spatial Moodboards
02Weeks 4–7

3D Spatial Modeling & Architecture

Our atelier renders full real-time 3D models of your rooms, exploring volumetric hierarchy, custom partition reveals, and ceiling datum alignments.

  • Interactive 3D Walkthroughs
  • Structural Architectural Plans
  • Lighting & Electrical Schema
03Weeks 8–14

Material Procurement & Craft

We travel directly to European stone quarries and master joineries to inspect travertine blocks, walnut slabs, and hand-loomed textiles in person.

  • Direct Quarry Block Selection
  • Joinery & Hardware Mockups
  • 1-of-1 Furniture Fabrication
04Weeks 15–18

White-Glove Turnkey Reveal

Our master installation crew oversees final acoustic tuning, sculpture placement, curated library styling, and fragrance orchestration for the handover.

  • Architectural Art Hanging
  • Acoustic & Lighting Balancing
  • Curated Archival Handover Dossier
